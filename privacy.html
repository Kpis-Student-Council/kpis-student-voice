Privacy Policy

What this site does with your information — plainly.

KPIS Student Voice lets KPIS International School students submit anonymous concerns and opinions to the Student Council, and like or reply to what others have shared.

Signing in

To submit, like, or reply, you sign in with your school Google account (@kpis.ac.th). This confirms you're a real KPIS student before your action is accepted.

What we do with your sign-in

  • Your school email is checked at the moment of each action (submitting, liking, replying) — never stored next to what you posted, and never shown to anyone, including the Student Council.
  • For likes specifically, a one-way scrambled version of your email (a "hash") is kept, only to stop the same person liking the same post twice. This scrambled version can't be reversed back into your actual email.
  • Beyond that check, your identity is not linked to your submissions, likes, or replies in any way.

What is stored

  • The text of submissions and replies, when they were posted, like counts, and pin status
  • The scrambled email hashes used to prevent duplicate likes (see above)

This is stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the site administrator (the Student Council webmaster) — never shown publicly.

Moderation

Administrators can remove any submission or reply, or pin one to the top, as outlined in the Community Guidelines.

Questions

Reach out to the KPIS Student Council with any questions about how this site handles your information.
