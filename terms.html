Community Guidelines

Please read before posting.

KPIS Student Voice exists so students can raise real concerns and opinions with the Student Council. It only works if people use it honestly and respectfully. By submitting, liking, or replying, you agree to the following:

Not allowed

  • No impersonation. Don't write as if you're someone else, or pretend a submission or reply comes from a specific person, teacher, or group.
  • No pointless or joke posts. This isn't a meme board — submissions and replies should be genuine concerns, opinions, or on-topic responses, not spam, filler, or jokes unrelated to school life.
  • No disrespectful or harmful content. No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or targeting specific students or staff by name in a demeaning way.
  • No attempts to bypass sign-in. Sign-in exists to keep this space genuine to KPIS students. Attempting to fake, share, or circumvent it isn't allowed.

What happens if these are broken

The Student Council reviews the site and can remove any submission or reply that breaks these guidelines, at their discretion. There's currently no automated ban system — moderation is done by a real person reading real posts. Repeated or serious misuse may be raised with school staff.

Anonymity still applies

Even though you sign in to post, your identity is never attached to what you submit, like, or reply to — see the Privacy Policy for details on exactly how that works.

Questions

Reach out to the KPIS Student Council with any questions about these guidelines.
