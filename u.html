Privacy Policy — KPIS Student Voice

This site lets KPIS International School students submit anonymous concerns and opinions to the Student Council.

Sign-in

To keep submissions genuine, students sign in with their school Google account (@kpis.ac.th) before submitting, liking, or replying. This confirms the person is a real KPIS student.

What we do with your Google sign-in

Your school email is used only at the moment of an action (submitting, liking, or replying) to confirm you're a KPIS student. It is not stored alongside your submission, not displayed publicly, and not linked to what you post. Submissions, likes, and replies remain fully anonymous to other students and to the Student Council.

What we do store

The text of submissions and replies, timestamps, like counts, and pin status are stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the site administrator.

Contact

Questions about this site can be directed to the KPIS Student Council.
